Bow Bridge

⤷ Quiet yet grand, iconic yet peaceful. Bow Bridge is the Central Park picture so many carry in their minds – its bowed cast iron gently clasping The Lake, with hints of buildings growing in the distance.

Bethesda Terrace & Fountain

⤷ Bethesda Terrace and Fountain are where Central Park turns into a stage. Every road leads here; people sit, pose, sing, give back massages, feed ducks, rowboat, panhandle, sketch, photograph, people watch.

Black and White

⤷ Some classic views of Central Park are made for black and white, creating a beauty and an aura that is tough to match.

Gapstow Bridge

⤷ Gapstow Bridge holds one of the most cinematic views in Central Park, the romantic curve of its stone, the grandness of its view pulls the scenery together.


Autumn

⤷ Autumn is when Central Park shows off–a peacock of a season. These prints focus on capturing these reds, yellows, and greens that dominate the fall season.

Literary (Poets’) Walk

⤷ Literary Walk is also known as The Mall, as well as Poets’ Walk, nicknamed for the surrounding statues of famous poets at its southern end.

Winter & Snow

⤷ In my youth, there was nothing more exciting than getting lost in Central Park in winter—the bigger the snowstorm, the better.

Night

⤷ Despite what you may have seen, Central Park at night is truly a relaxing and profound experience. The lights of skyscrapers make everything feel classic, & iconic.
