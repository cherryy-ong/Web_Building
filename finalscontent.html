.✦ ݁˖ Glass City

Glass City (2005–2025) is an expansive, two-decade longitudinal study that chronicles the relentless luxurification of Manhattan and the resulting fractures within the psyche of its long-term inhabitants. The project investigates the precarious existence of the creative neurodivergent survivor, navigating a sterile urban landscape increasingly forged from cold glass and predatory capital. By documenting these lived experiences, it reveals how individuals—often overlooked by the city's modern architecture—must radically improvise to maintain their dignity and forge a sense of belonging. Ultimately, the study highlights the resilient beauty found in the shadows and reflections of a metropolis designed to prioritize wealth over the human spirit.



HYPE ✦

HYPE: 10 Blocks, 10 Years, 10 Sneakers (2013-2024) is an alternative history book that interrogates the blurred lines between authenticity and artifice by tracing the evolution of SoHo and Canal Street. This decade-long exploration of Downtown NYC examines how the luxury sneaker, high-end brands, and cast-iron architecture became the physical manifestation of a new, influencer-driven hype culture. By navigating this hyper-commercialized landscape, the text reveals how the aggressive intersection of street style and global capital has fundamentally reshaped the city's social and physical identity. Ultimately, the project serves as a critical map of a neighborhood transformed into a global stage for consumption, where the objects we wear reflect the shifting soul of the urban environment.



.✦ ݁˖ All A Dream

All A Dream (2018–Current) is an ongoing, contemplative project that captures the spirit of Brooklyn through an intimate lens, documenting the transition of a lifelong Manhattanite as he navigates his new home. By focusing on nuanced architectural details and the quiet rhythm of the streets, the photographer uses the camera as a tool for cultural integration and personal discovery. The series seeks to move beyond surface-level observations, attempting to decode the unique visual language of the borough’s diverse neighborhoods. Through this persistent study, the work creates a poetic dialogue between the observer and his surroundings, finding profound meaning in the overlooked fragments of the urban landscape.



Speedway ✦

Speedway (2020–2023) offers a raw and kinetic look into an unlikely community space populated by a vivid spectrum of defiant and diverse personalities. The project explores the lives of those who exist on the margins of conventional society, choosing to live "beside freedom" in a world that often demands conformity. Through this lens, the work captures the unapologetic intensity of individuals who pursue their passions and personal truths with a relentless, high-octane energy. Ultimately, it serves as a powerful tribute to the human spirit, documenting a subculture of people who live life to the fullest until they finally cross the finish line.



.✦ ݁˖ New York City

New York City (2020-2024) captures the "concrete jungle" by harnessing the city’s extreme verticality and atmospheric light to convey immense scale. Its beauty lies in the contrast between geometric permanence and the neon fluidity of urban life. Through techniques like long exposures or capturing "Manhattanhenge," photographers transform chaotic streets into balanced compositions. Ultimately, this art form treats the city as a living character, immortalizing the moment where architecture and human energy converge.
